The Problem

Search friction and hallucinated AI answers present high risks in healthcare

🎤 Rania Mohamed (~1m)
📚
Information Overload

Medical information is vast and difficult to search quickly. Finding relevant evidence in dense clinical guidelines takes valuable time.

⚠
Generic AI Risks

Standard AI models generate answers from parametric memory, producing unsupported, ungrounded, or unverifiable medical information.

❌
High Risk in Healthcare

Hallucinated information in medical contexts is dangerous. Clinical questions require strict evidence, traceability, and verified sources.

🎯
Need for Grounding

Users require concise answers explicitly grounded in reliable evidence and directly traceable to clinical guidelines.

"How can we make AI-generated clinical answers more reliable and evidence-based?"

What Did We Build?

10-step end-to-end engineering pipeline & structured output schema

🎤 Rofaida Ashraf (~1.5m)
Complete 10-Step Pipeline
1. Document Ingestion (WHO & MedlinePlus Guidelines)
└─► 2. PDF / Medical Content Parsing
    └─► 3. Text Chunking (Chunk size=1000, overlap=150)
      └─► 4. Embedding Generation (BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5)
        └─► 5. ChromaDB Vector Storage
          └─► 6. Similarity Retrieval (Cosine Distance)
            └─► 7. Context Construction & Prompt Boundaries
              └─► 8. Gemini Grounded Generation
                └─► 9. Structured JSON Response Validation
                  └─► 10. UI Presentation (Streamlit Dashboard)
Enforced Output Schema
  • 🎯 Recommendation: Concise clinical answer
  • 📑 Evidence: Synthesized supporting text
  • 🔗 Citations: Document title & page number
  • 🛡 Confidence: HIGH / MEDIUM / INSUFFICIENT
